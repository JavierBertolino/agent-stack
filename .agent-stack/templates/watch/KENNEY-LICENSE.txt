The adjacent characters.json contains locally rendered views of the original
GLB models, not AI-generated substitutes. Source model filenames and animation
names are recorded in its sources array. Character order matches the watch
roles: resolver, designer, design-qa, developer, web-qa, mobile-qa, Jev.

Rebuild with scripts/render-watch-characters.mjs using an unpacked official
Mini Characters 2.0 download. Rendering uses development-only Three.js 0.181.1,
Playwright 1.56.1 and Chromium; none are dependencies of the watch viewer.
